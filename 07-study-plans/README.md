## 🎯 How to Follow a Plan

### Daily Routine
1. **Morning** (30-45 min): Review theory
2. **Afternoon** (1-2 hours): Solve problems
3. **Evening** (30 min): Review & revise

### Weekly Routine
- **Days 1-5**: Learn new concepts
- **Day 6**: Practice & consolidate
- **Day 7**: Mock interview or review

### Problem-Solving Approach
1. **Read** problem carefully (5 min)
2. **Think** about approach (10 min)
3. **Code** solution (20 min)
4. **Test** with examples (5 min)
5. **Optimize** if needed (10 min)
6. **Review** editorial (10 min)

## 💡 Tips for Success

### 1. Consistency Over Intensity
Better to do 1 hour daily than 7 hours once a week.

### 2. Understand, Don't Memorize
Focus on patterns and intuition, not solutions.

### 3. Revise Regularly
Review previous problems weekly to retain patterns.

### 4. Practice Mock Interviews
Simulate real interview conditions regularly.

### 5. Learn from Mistakes
When stuck, study the solution deeply.

### 6. Join Study Groups
Discuss problems with others for different perspectives.
